Frequently Asked Questions

Why are Vylex Nexys tools free?

We believe high-quality study tools, admission calculators, and science lab simulations should not be gated by paywalls or high subscription fees. Our mission is to level the academic playing field for all South African learners.

Can I donate with a South African card?

Yes! PayPal accepts all major South African debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Cheque cards) without needing a pre-existing PayPal account. Transactions are securely converted.

How are funds allocated?

100% of donations are reinvested into server hosting infrastructure, domain maintenance, research for university requirement databases, and developing new virtual science simulations.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. We do not store or process card information on our servers. All card and PayPal payments are processed through PayPal's secure 256-bit encrypted checkout, and crypto is handled directly peer-to-peer on the Bitcoin network.
