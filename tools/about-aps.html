About the APS Calculator

A free, public university admissions tool by Vylex Nexys, an education platform developed by Vylex (Pty) Ltd.

Why It Exists

Our mission for South African matriculants

Every year, hundreds of thousands of South African Grade 11 and Matric students face critical crossroads when applying to higher education institutions. Misunderstandings regarding Admission Point Scores (APS), faculty prerequisites (such as Core Mathematics versus Mathematical Literacy), and institutional differences often lead to rejected applications and lost opportunities.

This calculator was built to eliminate that friction. We believe that critical educational tools should be 100% free, transparent, fast, and accessible on any device—without paywalls, forced subscriptions, or data exploitation.

What the APS Calculator Does

Engineered for the South African National Senior Certificate (NSC / CAPS).

1

Multi-University Score Conversion

Instantly calculates your Standard NSC APS, UCT Admissions Score (excluding Life Orientation across your best 6 subjects), and Wits University APS (with Life Orientation capped).

2

What-If Upgrade Pathfinding

Analyzes your boundary marks and simulates the exact number of newly unlocked university degrees you gain by upgrading a subject by +1 Level.

3

Target Degree Gap Diagnostic

Pick a specific course to instantly audit your APS deficit/surplus, subject combination validity, and minimum mark benchmarks.

4

South African Bursary Matching

Connects your profile directly with verified national funding schemes including NSFAS, Allan Gray Orbis Foundation, Sasol STEM, and more.

Transparency & Privacy

Advisory Nature: The Vylex Nexys APS Calculator is designed as an educational planning aid. Minimum requirements and university thresholds are subject to annual adjustments by individual university faculties. Official selection often incorporates National Benchmark Tests (NBTs), limited program quotas, and portfolio submissions.

Privacy Commitment: Your academic marks are processed locally on your device. We do not store or sell student scores.
