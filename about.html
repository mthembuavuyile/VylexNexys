About Vylex Nexys

Practical web utilities built for South African learners

What Is Vylex Nexys?

Vylex Nexys is an educational technology ecosystem developed by Vylex Pty Ltd. We build clean, high-performance web applications—including university APS calculators, academic citation generators, distraction-free writing environments, interactive math suites, and virtual science laboratories—tailored for South African high school and university students.

Navigating Matric exams, university entry requirements, paper citations, and study schedules can often feel overwhelming. Vylex Nexys simplifies these tasks by offering clean, fast, and accessible tools that run directly in your web browser—no expensive software, account paywalls, or heavy downloads required.

Our Flagship App Suite

Core digital suites designed for everyday academic tasks

APS & Admissions Checker

Calculate your Admission Point Score (APS) from Grade 12 marks and verify eligibility across major South African university qualification thresholds.

SaCite Citation Builder

Format error-free APA, Harvard, MLA, Chicago, OSCOLA, and Vancouver reference citations in seconds for school essays and research papers.

Nexys Write & Lexico

A distraction-free academic writing studio paired with Lexico language intelligence, live readability stats, text cleaning, and speech synthesis.

Nexys Calc Math Suite

Interactive 5-in-1 math engine featuring function graphing, equation solving, numerical sequences, trigonometry, and triangle geometry.

Nexys Achieva Workspace

All-in-one student workspace featuring grade tracking, target feasibility calculations, automated study timetable generation, and focus timers.

VyLab Virtual Science Lab

CAPS curriculum-aligned virtual laboratory platform for running high school physical sciences (Physics & Chemistry) simulations online.
